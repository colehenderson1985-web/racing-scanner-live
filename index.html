Odds is TAB's current fixed price (Betfair exchange back/lay appears in yellow when that feed is connected). Arrows show the latest move. Rated is the scanner's win chance and the price that chance is worth. Edge compares the two: +15% means the price on offer pays 15% more than the scanner thinks it should. A runner is flagged VALUE when the edge clears 15% and the price is between $2.50 and $20 (short favourites and long shots are excluded on purpose). Place is the chance of running top three (top two in fields under 8). The speed map under each race groups runners by expected position early. The model is unvalidated: treat picks as a talking point, not advice.
